### Historical observations

Release 0.3.2, dated 2026-08-08, adopted the MIT license and public repository metadata after the owner accepted the audited historical PII exposure. The observations below retain their original dates and do not verify the current branch.

The visualisation layer was rewritten 2026-07-31, keeping the data model and every `lib/tree/` contract intact. What is new: light + dark + system themes on `[data-theme]` with a no-flash bootstrap (inline in Next, external under the SPA CSP) and a fresh contrast-scored token set; an in-app motion switch instead of `prefers-reduced-motion`; a person detail panel (side rail on a pointer device, bottom sheet on a phone) listing parents, partners with their union status, children, siblings, connections read from the subject's own end, channels-without-values and sources; DAG-safe expand/collapse in both directions with per-card hidden counts; a visited-history breadcrumb trail that rewinds rather than repeating; eleven keyboard shortcuts whose bindings and help sheet come from one array; and loading / error / alone / search-empty / offline states. `motion` (now v13) was added and is used for CHROME only -- the 117 cards and 150 edges stay CSS keyframes, because a JS animation re-renders a node and React Flow re-measures on render.

Two defects were found by writing the tests rather than by looking at the screen, and both are recorded in Gotchas: collapse blocking a person instead of their union (which renders a marriage with one participant), and a collapse walk seeded from the roots being unable to fold ancestors at all. A third was found by grepping the built CSS: this file's own gotcha note was compiling a dead Tailwind rule into production.

The connectors were rebuilt from scratch on 2026-07-31 and two more arrangements added, all recorded in Gotchas. Geometry moved to [lib/tree/paths.ts](lib/tree/paths.ts) with 14 tests: hard-corner edges went 82 of 150 -> 3 (the three are straight drops), and the 6013px sibling bar is gone. An ORBIT view landed alongside the pedigree ([lib/tree/radial.ts](lib/tree/radial.ts), 19 tests), centring one person with everybody else on rings by relationship distance -- it took four measured iterations to get right, and the lesson is in Gotchas: the tangle was the ANGLES (122 degree median span, 26 of 34 edges over 90) rather than the curves, and bowing them harder only made loops. Final geometry: 1 degree median span, 0 over 90, max radius 1680px, nodes 19/16/21/19 across the quadrants. A per-card 3D tilt was added and verified to keep hit-testing intact (13 of 14 visible tilted cards clickable at 151 tilted nodes).

Three more were found only by SCREENSHOTTING the canvas on 2026-07-31, after every per-element measurement had passed -- all three are in Gotchas. The living-status colour was painting 80% of every surface green (card rails, 93 of 152 minimap rects, 93 of 117 dots), the fold chevrons were 35px hit targets inside a scaled layer rather than 44, and the mobile detail sheet at 70dvh was covering the very card it described. Verified live afterwards on a clean reload at 1440x900 and 375x812, in both themes: 117 cards, 150 edges, ONE stroke treatment at rest, 0 amber family edges, 0 green rails, 0 relation edges at rest, 148 of 148 fold targets at 44 screen px across three zoom levels, no console errors, and no contact value anywhere in the detail panel (the only phone-shaped match in its text was the lifespan "1985 - 2025").

The canvas rework landed 2026-07-30 and was measured before and after in the browser, not reasoned about: the resting canvas went from 202 edges wearing 15 distinct stroke treatments to 150 edges wearing ONE, the 52 relation edges (73% of all edge ink, median span 2214px) became a per-person reveal, and the 68 amber partner edges went neutral. Confirmed live that clicking a person surfaces exactly their relations in the accent with the label showing, at desktop and 375px.

The schema is live on a real Neon branch as of 2026-07-29, and `loadTreeView()` was verified against it by a live smoke script, since removed (22 live checks: fusion across two graphs, kinship terms, contact filtering at `linked` access, mine-only, the no-graph case, and a pending link changing nothing). New-account provisioning was verified against the same branch: one graph, one self node with `sex` left `unknown`, `rootPersonId` set, a non-null `loadTreeView()` returning kinship "you", idempotent on a second call, and clean teardown. `/api/auth/providers` returns 200 with the callback on port 3007.

The public SPA is live at <https://sagargupta.online/kinfolk/> (Pages, serving the `frontend/` build), and the API plus server-rendered fallback are live at <https://kinfolk-neon.vercel.app>. Changes are logged in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
