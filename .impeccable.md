## Design Context

Prepared 2026-09-10 for the unpublished 0.4.0 branch.

### Users

Kinfolk is for relatives building a shared family record together, often in short sessions
on a phone and in longer research sessions on a desktop. They should be able to add a
parent, partner, child, sibling, or social connection without learning the graph schema.

### Brand Personality

Personal, trustworthy, clear. The product should feel like a carefully kept family album
with the clarity and confident typography of portfolio-react.

### Aesthetic Direction

Adapt portfolio-react's flat opaque surfaces to a family atlas: near-black `#0b1012`
in dark mode, blue `#2563eb` primary actions, and `#60a5fa` dark-mode accents.
Use locally served Bricolage Grotesque headings, Inter body text, JetBrains Mono metadata,
and flat borders. Provide both light and dark themes.
A simple Tree / People workspace and generous reading space keep family content dominant.
Motion explains a branch appearing, a person opening, or two records connecting.
Full motion is the default, with a persistent explicit Reduced option in Appearance.
Reduced keeps every task available and removes animated canvas travel.

### Design Principles

1. Select a person, then act on that branch.
2. Relationship words lead; database concepts stay behind the interface.
3. Private by default, with every wider audience requiring an explicit decision.
4. Mobile keeps every core action within thumb reach rather than hiding functionality.
5. Shared components must work in both the Next and Vite frontends.
